# Essay 5 Seed: "What I Can't See"
### Parked. The steelman essay. Follows essay 4's fourth-wall break, continues personal voice. Closes the five-essay arc: thesis → warning → prescription → embodiment → limits.

**The symmetry (why this essay must exist).** The publication is *What the Map Misses*. Essay 5 asks what the author's map misses. Four essays arguing the model can't see the terrain; the fifth asks what I can't see. This is the epistemics that makes the series (and book) trustworthy to serious reviewers. It converts the steelman from defensive obligation into the natural next act of the personal voice.

**The steelman at full strength (no punches pulled).** Polanyi's floor is defined by what's unwritten — but "unwritten" quietly meant "unrecorded." World models + continuous audio/video ingestion + ambient agents in every meeting and thread attack the floor at its foundation: the tacit layer was never metaphysically inaccessible, just economically unrecorded. Sharpest form: *tacit knowledge might be an artifact of the cost of observation, and observation is getting cheap.* If true, the vintner's monopoly is a temporary information asymmetry, not a structural one — and the series has an expiration date.

**Integrity constraint (non-negotiable).** No pre-installed escape hatch. The weak version steelmans for six paragraphs then reveals the trapdoor. The strong version genuinely doesn't know for a stretch, then earns what survives. All defenses below are marked PROVISIONAL — present them as "where I currently think the floor holds," with honest edges. "Here's where my thesis erodes first" is the most credible sentence in the book.

**Provisional defenses (where the floor may hold):**
- *The counterfactual gap.* Observation captures behavior; much of the tacit layer is counterfactual — the memo never sent, the meeting deliberately not called, why a thing DOESN'T happen. Cameras record what occurred; terrain knowledge includes what was avoided. Avoidance leaves no data.
- *The relocation effect (Lucas-critique shaped).* The moment institutions know they're ambiently recorded, the real conversations move. The tacit layer migrates off-camera — that's what tacit layers do. Surveillance doesn't capture the terrain; it relocates it.
- *The consent wall.* Unlike the dynamo, ambient ingestion requires institutional consent to observation. Diffusion friction the capability curve can't route around.

**The resolution (the essay's actual conclusion — dominance argument).** Two worlds: floor holds, or floor erodes. If it holds: vintner keeps the monopoly, architect prospers by investing it. If it erodes: the last labor moat drains, and ONLY ownership still pays — ownership prices outcomes, never needed the moat. Build the loom wins in both worlds. The steelman doesn't threaten the thesis's conclusion; it threatens its comfort. Honest edge to state plainly: paths one and two (middle seat, foreman) are conditional on the floor holding; path three never was. If the floor falls, the foreman path evaporates with it. "No judgment" survives; the clock behind the fork gets louder.

**Timeline calibration (GPT-3 → Fable).** Humility cut: autocomplete to frontier reasoning in ~3-4 years — nobody gets to assume the hurdle holds, least of all the writer who opened essay one with "a year ago this was contested; it isn't anymore." Counterweight cut: capability jumps and diffusion are different clocks. The essay's own dynamo story — electricity took thirty years to reach productivity because institutions redesign slower than technology improves. Ambient ingestion faces the same lag PLUS the consent wall and relocation effect. Even under aggressive capability timelines, the diffusion gap is years — and that gap is precisely the rail-switching window.

**Closing logic (writes itself).** I can't tell you the floor holds forever. I can tell you the window is open now, that it closes from the bottom of the stack up, and that the one position robust to my being wrong is the one I've been prescribing all along.

**Book function.** This is the chapter that determines whether the book gets respected or merely liked. With it, the five-essay arc is closed and the book becomes finishable: remaining gaps (craft section, pricing essay, enterprise flip-side/GPRA chapter, failure stories) are chapters inside a finished argument, not extensions of an open one.
