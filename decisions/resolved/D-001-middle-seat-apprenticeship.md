**Scope:** Current author position, open to later explicit revision. Acceptance establishes the intended argument, not empirical efficacy or a frozen thesis.

## Current author position

The developmental sequence is **manual fundamentals sufficient to understand what happens under the hood → informed use of tools and abstractions → advanced judgment through actual supervised work**. Manual foundational understanding is part of the position. Its exact amount, form and pacing across domains remain curriculum and research questions; it does not require preserving every obsolete production task forever.

The middle seat names the human knowledge-work role in **AI prepares → human judges → AI finalizes**. Masters and apprentices can both occupy it, with different experience, supervision and authority. Occupancy alone is not apprenticeship.

Apprenticeship continues inside real work: a junior makes a genuine first judgment; a senior or other appropriate consequential authority reviews it; feedback and increasing responsibility develop capability. This includes exceptions and edge cases, assumptions, novel combinations, situational fit, consequences and decision ownership appropriate to the person's authority. It is not a mandatory separate pre-work program. Experienced people can still learn from unfamiliar cases without erasing differences in expertise or authority.

Production redesign can organize routine execution around reusable, tested capabilities composed by AI. Human learning then includes judging those capabilities' assumptions, combinations, applicability and consequences. Their bounds must be established, not presumed. This is not a claim that AI can never handle exceptions.

Tool-use educators and trainers build the human capital needed to use AI tools and workflows. Their work complements supervised development of consequential judgment but is distinct from it. Experienced operators becoming foremen or mentors represent career progression within operation, not another independent value-capture path. One person may perform both activities.

## Explicit revision and preserved conflict

The original [decision packet](../pending/D-001-middle-seat-apprenticeship.md) preserves the opposing claims and their source locators. Its unresolved framing records the Loop 1 state rather than the present decision.

- C-018's station-to-foreman progression survives only conditionally: the station must contain substantive judgment, supervision, feedback and increasing responsibility. Ordinary station occupancy does not reliably reproduce expertise by itself.
- The blanket claim that reviewing AI output severs apprenticeship is narrowed. Passive approval and active, supervised judgment are different activities.
- The seed-corn warning remains: automating production without preserving meaningful practice, feedback and responsibility can undermine future expertise.
- The accepted answer develops the packet's third synthesis, with an explicit manual-foundations commitment and the clarification that apprenticeship can remain within real knowledge work.

The historical sources remain unchanged: S-03's station progression, S-02's underlying practice and senior proximity, S-05's review-only warning and builder pathway, and S-10 §5's deliberate redesign. Exact paths and quotations remain in the original packet and claim ledger.

## Evidence and implementation still required

**R-08** remains open: which experiences develop transferable, independent judgment; the amount, form and pacing of manual foundational practice; whether tool-use instruction transfers into capable work; whether supervision, correction and real responsibility survive production pressure; and which tests establish the limits of reusable capabilities. R-07 and R-09 address authority, workload and institutional incentives.

Research may challenge efficacy and inform a later explicit author revision. It does not substitute a new author choice for the accepted foundational-understanding requirement.
