# D-001 — Does middle-seat work reproduce the judgment it needs?

**Status:** PENDING — AUTHOR_DECISION_REQUIRED. Loop 1 identifies the fork and does not select a resolution.

## 1. Decision ID

D-001. Audit A-04; claims C-015–C-019 and C-021.

## 2. Exact crux

Does ordinary AI-in/human-review/AI-out station work supply the developmental experience that turns a junior into a territory-rich foreman, or does it remove that experience unless the institution deliberately adds another training pathway?

## 3. Position A — Station work can be the apprenticeship

C-018; S-03, `source/retired/03-portugal-didnt-have-to-lose.md`, “The foreman: where the gray beards sit”: “Work the stations well, accumulate the terrain knowledge, and the seat can grow into the foreman's job.” The same passage says early/mid-career workers staff stations and that the seat can mature into the foreman for those who treat it as apprenticeship. “The decision” repeats the promotion path. This is retired material, but no passage explicitly withdraws this particular mechanism.

## 4. Position B — Reviewing AI output is not an adequate developmental pipeline

C-015/C-016/C-019; S-02, `source/essays/02-dont-eat-your-seed-corn.md`, agentic-work comparison and training passages: “how would you know which ones are subtly wrong if you've never sat in the rooms where they get acted on?” It requires reps doing the underlying cognitive work, senior proximity and real stakes. S-05, `source/drafts/essay-4-seed-simultaneous-specialization.md`, “The seed corn reversal”: “The operator who only reviews AI output has his apprenticeship severed.” S-10 §5, `source/synthesis/2026-09-11-current-author-synthesis.md`, requires deliberate redesign rather than assuming expertise continues after the old pathway disappears.

## 5. Why both cannot survive unchanged

The same review-centered station cannot simultaneously be the default junior-to-expert production pathway and the activity that severs that pathway. S-03 does not specify the additional reps, exposure or authority that would distinguish its developmental station from S-02/S-05's inadequate reviewer. Reading those conditions into it would change its prescription. This is not a claim that no station could ever teach; it is a conflict over what the book may assume ordinary station work accomplishes.

## 6. If A is chosen

The book must explain which experiences in station work develop judgment and qualify the review-severs-apprenticeship claim. The labor arc can retain an endogenous station-to-foreman progression, supported by evidence rather than the Ford analogy alone.

## 7. If B is chosen

The book must withdraw or explicitly condition S-03's default promotion mechanism. The training architecture is a necessary institutional investment; an AI review job cannot be presented to juniors as sufficient apprenticeship merely because it is called a middle seat.

## 8. Is a third synthesis available?

Yes, as an author-approved revision: distinguish review-only stations from deliberately developmental stations with underlying cognitive reps, situated exposure, senior feedback and escalating stakes. S-02, S-05 and S-10 supply ingredients, but the corpus does not establish that S-03's station already contains them. This packet does not adopt the synthesis or specify its efficacy.

## 9. Evidence that could help

R-08: compare learning and independent judgment across review-only, active reconstruction, AI tutor/workshop and supervised real-work pathways. Sector and task differences matter. Evidence could establish training conditions; Luke must decide which mechanism the book intends and how to revise the conflicting passages.

Should the book retain middle-seat work itself as a route to expert judgment, require a separate deliberate apprenticeship pathway, or distinguish explicitly designed developmental stations from review-only stations?
