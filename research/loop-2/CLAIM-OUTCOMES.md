## How to read this matrix

The [Loop 1 ledger](../../evidence/claims/CLAIM-LEDGER.md) is historical provenance. Its former pending classifications do not supersede the accepted, revisable [D-001](../../decisions/resolved/D-001-middle-seat-apprenticeship.md) and [D-002](../../decisions/resolved/D-002-ownership-dominance.md). This is a separate research disposition, not an amendment to those artifacts or a thesis adoption. See the [analytical synthesis](CROSS-PROGRAM-SYNTHESIS.md) for dependencies and author refinement questions.

“Supported” is always bounded: a possibility, mechanism, definition, textual report or normative rationale is not automatically an observed average effect. “Revised/qualified” separates surviving clauses from stronger unsupported ones. “Contradicted” applies only to the specified claim, inference or universal. “Unresolved” is not a finding of no effect. A normative **should** is not empirically proved; an authored definition can be accurately reported while conflicting with standard terminology. Auxiliary tests and stronger predecessor passages remain distinct from each row's exact C-proposition.

The columns identify the claim's epistemic role/current author state, then synthesize clause-level outcomes. Source-owning ledgers and substantive sections carry exact source populations, estimates, versions, method and access limits. No numbers are pooled and a shared study is not independent evidence each time it is referenced. A complete review can leave empirical uncertainty; an unaccepted review remains a completion gate.
