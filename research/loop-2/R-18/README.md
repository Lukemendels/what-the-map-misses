## What this review establishes

The historical argument needs substantial qualification, but the research does not reduce to a list of debunked anecdotes. Across otherwise different literatures, a more useful problem emerges: how technologies, skills, complementary assets, market access, institutions and control rights jointly affect productive possibilities and distribute gains. That problem fits the book's research agenda without requiring history to provide a universal script.

The claim that one input becomes more productive does not settle whether a whole process improves. Improvement in a process does not settle whether a worker acquires expertise. Useful expertise does not determine its holder's compensation. A valuable asset does not guarantee a return to whoever financed it. National growth does not describe the experience of every participant. These are distinct transitions requiring distinct evidence. The component reviews test them rather than carrying a verified date or famous success across every transition.

This program contains four substantial, integrated literature reviews. Read them as complementary parts of one historical audit, not as four independent proofs of the book's thesis:

1. [Portugal, Ricardo and Methuen](portugal.md): primary trade reasoning and treaty wording; rival diplomatic, developmental, political-power, resource-curse and structural histories; wine-sector assets and networks; proposition-level verdicts.
2. [Ford, wages, workweeks, Jevons and surplus narratives](ford-surplus.md): production measurement and chronology; competing labor/compensation mechanisms; labor institutions; conditional demand responses; cross-technology tests of the phase story.
3. [Knowledge burdens, biographies and electrification](biography-electricity.md): Jones's mechanism and proxies; biographies versus causal evidence; competing electricity diffusion and productivity findings.
4. [AI buildout and historical investment booms](buildout.md): the requested news-to-paper trace, calculation audit, measurement comparability, and scholarly debates about infrastructure, network benefits, financing and returns.

Each component contains its own claim-level ledger, recoverable bibliography, source-access record, method limitations and investigated gaps. Source IDs are scoped by component. A shared source is not independent corroboration; the David–Wright discussion is cross-linked rather than repeated. Original authored files and the historical Loop 1 ledger remain unchanged.

## Integrative literature assessment

### Exchange, development and capture are different questions

The Portugal literature is most productive when the question is decomposed. A conditional exchange calculation concerns relative opportunity costs. A treaty concerns specific access rules. Development histories concern technologies, sectors, institutions and counterfactual trajectories. Business histories concern actors positioned within production and distribution networks. Substituting any one of these for all the others turns an instructive comparison into a morality tale.

This distinction matters beyond Portugal. Owning an output-producing asset, supplying scarce skill, maintaining a relationship and controlling access to a market can produce different income and control arrangements. National labels conceal heterogeneous actors. The same technology can benefit a consumer and undermine an investor; a firm may create useful output while a particular employee captures little of its value. R-05, R-12 and R-15 must therefore examine mechanisms of compensation and control rather than inherit a historical winner's label.

### Productive redesign is a hypothesis about complements, not a calendar

The electricity and transport literatures disagree in part because they study different outcomes and counterfactuals. Engineering descriptions identify possibilities; reconstructed aggregate series describe diffusion and growth; local or industry exposure strategies try to isolate responses; network models account for benefits that cross the apparent treatment boundary. A result at one level need not cancel a result at another, but neither can be silently generalized.

For the book, the appropriate inference is a research design: compare feasible production arrangements on completed, quality-adjusted work and full costs. Historical complementarity makes that comparison worth performing. It does not decide in advance whether model quality, workflow, power, capital replacement, management, demand or another constraint is binding. R-04 inherits the competing evidence and these distinctions, not a certified delay period.

### Learning, performance and the distribution of opportunities must stay separate

Biographies can reveal possible arrangements of work, practice and access. They cannot by themselves establish a population effect or the cause of an exceptional person's achievement. The relevant counterfactual may be another tool, institution, collaborator or career path, rather than an imagined person without any assistance. Selecting a celebrated outcome and reasoning backward misses the people who had similar exposure without comparable results.

Jones's innovation framework and the biographical cases therefore motivate R-08 and R-10's distinctions among assisted production, retained knowledge, transfer, frontier contribution and commercial reach. Neither a successful artifact nor exposure to an expert is an adequate proxy for all five. The accepted D-001 supplies an author position about foundations and supervised work; research must still evaluate its mechanisms and scope.

### Investment, useful services and financial outcomes require separate ledgers

The requested AI-buildout comparison has a recoverable provenance and a checkable narrow arithmetic claim. Its detailed capsule and limits are in the buildout review, without repetition here. The larger methodological lesson is that expenditures measure commitments, not the resulting benefits. Historical asset boundaries, price bases, time windows and contemporary scenario assumptions matter before a ranking is meaningful.

Private returns and social usefulness can also diverge. A financing loss need not imply useless infrastructure, and useful infrastructure does not imply efficient spending or well-compensated labor. A historical resemblance may identify which contracts and exposures to inspect; it cannot establish a future crash, guaranteed prosperity, or a universal surplus sequence. R-11 must preserve the accounting boundaries and R-12 must supply the aggregation and distribution mechanisms.

## What remains genuinely unresolved

The unresolved items are not a backlog disguised as completed research. The components describe the relevant searches, inspected source types, access limits, competing methods and evidence needed to decide each question. Important examples include matched historical vineyard-productivity measures; comparative uses of export receipts; a treaty-specific development counterfactual; robust present-day learning transfer from expert access; harmonized historical investment perimeters; and replication of research-vintage project data not publicly recovered.

No paywall was bypassed, paid dataset bought, account created, private author origin investigated or third party contacted. Public sources and the admitted public corpus were the sole inputs. Unavailable source versions are identified as such, and a preliminary manuscript is not silently represented as a final publication.
