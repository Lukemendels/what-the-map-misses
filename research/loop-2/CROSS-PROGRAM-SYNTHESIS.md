## 1. Integrated result: a conditional argument survives, not a historical law

The reviews support taking the coupled production system seriously. Capability and input price alone do not determine useful output. The available information, software, task boundaries, human activities, permissions, checks, incentives and complements can change whether a technical capability becomes a dependable service. There is also credible evidence that interventions can improve some of these components, sometimes at the level of an implemented workflow rather than merely a benchmark.

This conclusion is consequential without being universal. Useful task acceleration need not await organizational reconstruction. A more elaborate system can add cost, coordination failures and common-mode error. Some projects remain uneconomical even when generation is cheap. Comparative returns to different degrees of redesign are not established across representative organizations. C-005's **can** survives; claims that **most** failures arise from inherited layouts, or that redesign always dominates model improvement, do not inherit its support. [R-01, useful-task economics](R-01/README.md#1-the-economically-relevant-unit); [R-02, lifecycle threshold](R-02/README.md#minimum-viable-software-is-a-lifecycle-threshold); [R-04, bottlenecks and acceleration](R-04/README.md#4-bottlenecks-completion-and-the-false-choice-between-acceleration-and-redesign).

The most important cross-program result is the separation of transitions that the earlier rhetoric sometimes compresses:

1. A cheaper input can improve a feasible workflow, but full costs and useful completion must be established.
2. Better assisted output can coexist with weaker, stronger or unchanged independent human capability.
3. Valuable architecture, education or operation need not command proportional compensation.
4. A persistent and copyable state need not preserve useful capability, lawful access or economic advantage after transfer.
5. More investment, production or market revenue does not independently establish net welfare or equitable gains.

These are not five findings of failure. They identify where positive mechanisms need additional evidence, which qualifications already have substantive support, and where the remaining choice is normative. They also prevent a retreat from an unsupported guarantee into an untestable statement that everything depends on everything: each review names the particular missing transition and its relevant counterfactual.

C-053 therefore remains a coherent, research-informed candidate synthesis, supported in important components and bounded by unresolved effects. Neither the number of reviews nor recurrence of a mechanism in several disciplines independently proves the composite claim. Its human-development and authority priorities contain value judgments. Its future adaptive-capital extension is conditional and must not become a prerequisite silently assumed in the present-day argument.

## 2. Shared vocabulary and outcome boundaries

A synthesis cannot reconcile studies by giving unlike quantities one convenient name. The following distinctions govern the comparisons below. They are analytical conventions for reading the research, not newly adopted author terminology.

| Expression | Required distinction | Consequence for the argument |
|---|---|---|
| Cognition becoming cheap | Posted inference price, fixed-benchmark capability cost, frontier-performance expenditure, and full cost per useful completion | Cheaper access is better supported than a representative useful-task price trend; more tokens do not measure more valuable cognition. |
| Software becoming viable | Demonstration, bounded local instrument, operational service and institutional system | A short-lived script can be worthwhile without enterprise obligations; a small deployment can nevertheless have high stakes. |
| Production function | Workflow architecture; feasible production set/frontier; realized outcome; estimated input–output relation | Organization can enter formal economics without equating a workflow diagram with the formal function. “Writable” may remain an explicit metaphor. |
| New factor | Newly provisionable service, separately modeled input, produced asset, or irreducible primary factor | Novel productive usefulness does not establish a new fundamental accounting category. |
| Learning | Assisted performance, immediate independent acquisition, retention, transfer and advanced judgment | The assistance present and the follow-up interval are part of every educational claim. |
| Human contribution | Technical capability, actual influence, legitimate authority, assigned duty, blame and legal liability | Neither accuracy nor an approval log settles all these questions. |
| Value and capital | Resource cost, stock, service flow, revenue, net returns, rents and welfare | Avoid both zero-price/zero-value reasoning and adding overlapping benefits to GDP. |
| Persistent experience | External records, executable skills, in-context state, saved parameter adaptation, and administrative permissions | Useful continuity can exist without weight learning; permission changes are not RLHF. |
| Portability | Technical reproduction, legal permission, operational compatibility and economic performance | A copied component or export button proves only a limited part of the proposed asset transfer. |

The production-function correction is substantive but need not flatten the author's insight. Reorganization can move an institution toward an existing frontier, select another feasible technique, expand its practical options, or create a new technical possibility. A better output definition can also change the measured object. Those are distinct gains; they can occur together. Improving one firm's access to an existing technique is different from expanding the world's technological frontier. Formal models of organization already accommodate information-processing structure, complementary choices and innovation, with explicit limits. [R-03 §§2–4](R-03/README.md#2-from-workflow-to-production-possibilities).

“Completed work” must likewise name a terminal state. A rated proposal, accepted pull request, maintained application, authorized decision and improvement in a beneficiary's condition are not the same outcome. Quality, effort, elapsed time, coverage, serious failure and downstream correction should remain separately visible when no defensible single index exists. Counting only successful attempts or treating rework shifted to another person as a saving would distort the comparison. [R-01 §1](R-01/README.md#1-the-economically-relevant-unit); [R-04 §4.3](R-04/README.md#43-completed-quality-adjusted-work-is-a-measurement-discipline).

The horizons also differ. Immediate task results cannot validate occupational apprenticeship; a surviving record cannot establish years of beneficial adaptation; short-run firm pay cannot identify a lifetime distributional outcome. Historical innovation, feasible adoption, individual installation, reorganization and peak aggregate impact are different clocks. None supplies a calibrated schedule for AI. An explicit interval makes “durable” testable; “permanent” and unbounded “eventually” are much stronger claims.

## 3. From provisionable cognition to useful production

### The cost and demand premises are directional, heterogeneous and incomplete

R-01 establishes the strongest supply-side case at fixed benchmark capability levels. It also explains why frontier performance can become more expensive while older capability becomes cheaper. These are different quality targets, not inconsistent estimates. Study periods, reasoning budgets, model selection and pricing reconstruction change the apparent rate. Their source-specific figures remain in [R-01's price capsules](R-01/README.md#2-what-the-price-evidence-actually-measures), rather than being restated here.

The economic threshold additionally depends on specification, authorized inputs, verification, integration, latency, maintenance and expected use before obsolescence. Some activities cross it; others do not. Existing assistance can increase depth within an activity, while new activities can become worth attempting. Neither intensive nor extensive expansion is measured by provider switching or verbosity alone. Direct platform-demand estimates disagree under different reconstructions, and neither identifies long-run economy-wide demand for verified services. This is an unresolved empirical disagreement, not a vote to average or an excuse to choose the more congenial coefficient. [R-01 §6](R-01/README.md#6-demand-expansion-is-conditional).

The software evidence sharpens the extensive-margin problem. Experiments on already-employed developers' assigned work need not identify whether additional domain specialists now create useful tools. Conversely, participation records and functioning prototypes need not identify whether those tools would otherwise have been uneconomical relative to spreadsheets, configured platforms, existing services or conventional support. The no-build alternative can be real; avoided procurement expenditure then misstates the benefit. R-02 supplies evidence of bounded gains, a genuine maintainer slowdown and wider participation, but not a population estimate of net lifecycle additionality. [R-02, controlled comparisons](R-02/README.md#controlled-gains-real-slowdowns-and-incompatible-denominators); [R-10, community building](R-10/README.md#8-historical-novelty-and-the-community-builder-mechanism).

### Redesign and acceleration are different interventions, not opposing camps

The older electrification story makes a real complementarity mechanism visible, but its universal delay and exclusive layout explanation do not survive. Local gains can arrive early while national diffusion remains slow; engineering opportunities and cross-country productivity paths need not agree at the same date. Similar distinctions apply now: a bounded task can improve immediately, while a different workflow still waits for data, permission, review capacity or demand. R-04's implemented-workflow examples are informative precisely because they specify the intervention and actual control. Most estimate a package, not the separately identified contributions of model, code, people and architecture. [R-18, electricity](R-18/biography-electricity.md#5-electrification-the-mechanism-survives-the-simple-delay-story-does-not); [R-04 §§3–5](R-04/README.md#3-complementarity-mechanism-evidence-and-measurement).

The proposed division between generation, deterministic operations and human acceptance is technically plausible and locally useful. A deterministic program can execute the wrong specification correctly; a human can approve an unexamined result. The model/code/human labels do not confer system reliability. AutoReviewer remains first-person testimony after a bounded unsuccessful public corroboration search. Analogous technical results support testing its mechanism, not verification of its history or a general causal effect. [R-04 §6](R-04/README.md#6-autoreviewer-testimony-technical-plausibility-and-missing-identification).

Models helping build their surrounding software is an actual feasibility result, distinct from weight-level self-improvement. Its loop can retain better evaluators and tools, or propagate a shared misunderstanding through implementation, tests and review. Reliable compounding and net multiyear return remain unestablished. Similarly, recurring redesign can maintain fit or generate churn. There must be a credible condition under which keeping the existing arrangement is the better choice; otherwise disappointing results can always be excused by demanding another redesign. [R-02, endogenous construction](R-02/README.md#endogenous-construction-a-genuine-loop-with-an-unmeasured-net-effect); [R-05, dynamic capability](R-05/README.md#dynamic-capabilities-versus-reproducible-good-practice).

## 4. Human capability: formation, current use and compensation

### The learning disagreement becomes intelligible after the treatment is specified

The apprenticeship concern survives as a risk mechanism. Some assistance designs improve what learners deliver while reducing what they independently demonstrate afterward. Other designed tutoring and professional-development packages improve measured learning. These results do not cancel: answer delivery, structured feedback, additional instructional time, learner-generated attempts and supervised practice are different treatments. Immediate tests, delayed retention and unfamiliar-case transfer are different endpoints. No reviewed longitudinal study validates the entire D-001 sequence across occupations, determines a universal manual dosage, or establishes broad judgment transfer from AI-assisted building. [R-08 §§2–5](R-08/README.md#2-the-outcomes-must-be-separated-before-comparing-interventions).

This supports D-001's distinction between seat occupancy and substantive apprenticeship, without claiming the accepted design is already proved. An active reviewer who diagnoses, explains and receives correction may perform genuine developmental work. A builder who accepts complete generated solutions may not. “Reviewer” and “builder” are not sufficient descriptions of cognitive activity. Foundational practice should be connected to later recognition, diagnosis, reconstruction and escalation, while the required amount and timing remain domain-sensitive. Tool-use education is a productive activity with a proximal outcome; it neither equals nor necessarily substitutes for advanced supervised judgment. [R-08 §6](R-08/README.md#6-a-testable-interpretation-of-d-001); [R-10 §§4,7](R-10/README.md#4-prerequisites-cognitive-load-and-transfer).

Real consequences alone are not an infallible teacher. Feedback can be delayed, ambiguous, selected or misleading. Senior intervention may preserve current output without revealing the junior's error. Simulation can teach transferable components without establishing whole-role judgment or replacing responsibility allocation. Preserving development therefore does not entail preserving every obsolete task, every old job structure or every current senior indefinitely. It entails identifying what capability is needed, how it is acquired and how the proposed replacement is assessed.

### Senior knowledge is a conditional investment problem

R-09 distinguishes present correction, future capability formation and who pays or gains. Specificity is graded across tasks, sectors, firms and relationships; it is not absolute nontransferability. Unexpected departures, planned retirements, external recruitment, coworker substitution and relationship disruption identify different treatments. A reduction in one firm's access is not necessarily destruction of an economy-wide stock. A cohort's aging is not a net-capability time series. [R-09 §§2–4](R-09/README.md#2-what-is-specific-and-to-what).

The seed-corn warning is strongest where a budget counts current output but misses effective mentoring, successor competence or recovery capacity. It is not evidence of universal managerial ignorance: firms can rationally train transferable workers, face financing or appropriability constraints, mismeasure returns, or correctly stop investing in obsolete capability. The causes require different remedies. Contemporary hiring patterns can identify an entry-route concern without demonstrating that AI caused a future expertise collapse. The Ford personnel report supplies attributed complementarity, not the missing complete causal chain from AI dismissals through skill loss to costly rehiring. [R-09 §§5–8](R-09/README.md#5-why-a-firm-might-train-undertrain-or-reduce-capacity).

This has a production implication beyond education. If the current workflow depends on future reviewers, training is part of reproducing a productive complement. Pricing today's human review without checking its renewal can understate long-run cost. Conversely, attributing every current expert's value to indispensable teaching can overstate it. The two functions require separate outcomes, and teaching time can reduce an expert's measured individual throughput while raising team capability.

### Breadth can expand without abolishing specialization

Acquisition burdens are one explanation of specialization, not an alternative to cognitive constraints: attention, prerequisites and transfer difficulty help create acquisition costs. Cheaper learning and cheaper consultation can also move organization in opposite directions. One encourages broader internal competence; the other may make it economical to learn less and refer more. Faster growth of the frontier can offset easier access. The evidence supports some assisted functional breadth and narrower learning gains, not a general reversal of specialization or a single judgment specialty transferable across arbitrary domains. [R-10 §§2–6](R-10/README.md#2-what-would-count-as-a-partial-reversal).

Local user knowledge and accessible building tools have important predecessors. The contemporary question is their changed feasibility, cost and distribution, rather than whether integration first became valuable. Productive scope, retained expertise, frontier innovation, collective diversity and commercial diffusion should remain separate. Broader individual output can coexist with homogenized ideas or continuing dependence on specialists. The source-owning [P&G and BCG capsules](R-10/README.md#6-direct-ai-evidence-on-productive-breadth-and-its-limits) are therefore not evidence of retained multi-domain mastery or generic-team replacement.

## 5. Situated knowledge, meaningful authority and fallible institutions

### A changing capability boundary does not settle the human place

R-06 preserves genuine philosophical disagreement about tacit knowing. It also rejects the operational inference from incomplete verbal articulation to zero machine-learnable evidence. Demonstration, interaction and alternative production methods can reproduce useful functions without exhaustively extracting a person's experience. This does not prove complete capture of institutional judgment or resolve phenomenal understanding. Unrecorded, inarticulable, embodied, relational and counterfactual knowledge are not interchangeable. [R-06 §§2–6](R-06/README.md#2-what-the-contested-words-mean).

The present possibility in C-011 survives: a particular human can supply decision-relevant information, relationships or consequence-sensitive understanding missing from a particular model workflow. Its prevalence, comparative advantage and economic magnitude remain case-specific. Observation must become usable evidence, learning and sustained action; richer sensors do not automatically reveal causes or legitimate access. Counterfactual absence can be informative but insufficient for identification. Surveillance can change disclosure beneficially or harmfully. Consent and information-flow restrictions are institutional and normative constraints, not a guaranteed technical refuge for human scarcity. C-010 remains superseded; C-047 remains genuinely provisional. [R-06 §§5–9](R-06/README.md#5-machines-can-learn-from-more-than-articulated-rules).

Comparative advantage supplies no substitute monopoly proof. The predecessor's absolute-advantage condition is incorrect, and a widening absolute gap need not alter relative opportunity costs. But finite-capacity exchange reasoning is not a promise of employment at an unchanged wage when machine services can be provisioned more broadly. A human-only input would still need demand, scarcity and bargaining to generate high compensation. Capability, productive necessity, economic leverage and legitimate authority require separate arguments. [R-03 §5](R-03/README.md#5-comparative-advantage-under-the-conditions-that-actually-matter); [R-18, Ricardo](R-18/portugal.md#ricardos-actual-demonstration).

### Approval must be assessed as an effective institutional capacity

C-014 is a conditional possibility, not a measured prevalence claim. Review can become ineffective through inadequate attention, information, expertise, staffing, incentives or intervention powers. Evidence of automation bias is not evidence that every human invariably obeys machine advice; human disagreement can itself introduce error. Explanation can improve one outcome and impair another. A high acceptance rate, override rate or confidence score cannot stand in for effective agency. [R-07 §§2–5](R-07/README.md#2-why-review-can-become-a-different-and-harder-job).

Meaningful authority may be exercised at deployment, policy, exception or individual-case level. Human placement need not be a final click on every output. The relevant test is whether appropriate people can understand the decision boundary, obtain necessary information, change or stop the relevant process, secure support and provide remedy. Responsibility assigned after a failure must not be confused with causal control, justified blame or adjudicated liability. Legal oversight requirements also do not demonstrate operational efficacy; R-07's legal illustration is jurisdiction- and version-bound. [R-07 §§6–9](R-07/README.md#6-authority-is-a-property-of-the-institution-as-well-as-the-interface).

D-001 and D-002 depend on these practical conditions in different ways. A station with genuine judgment and feedback may educate; a station with consequential influence may contribute to current quality; neither condition alone establishes the other or guarantees compensation. Production pressure can crowd out both review and explanation. Protecting an educational role and protecting decision authority are distinct institutional choices even when one person occupies both.

### Institutional design mitigates specified failures and introduces others

The strongest reliability mechanisms are bounded: external enforcement of represented predicates, tests of specified properties, independently captured actions, conditional error detection and repair matched to fault boundaries. Determinism does not supply truthful premises; agreement among critics does not establish independent evidence; a trace does not reveal complete internal reasoning. A record can preserve a false lesson. Fast local workarounds can keep production moving while concealing causes that require institutional change. [R-13 §§2–7](R-13/README.md#2-what-a-reliability-claim-must-specify).

The V3/V4 evolution should also remain precise. A V3 component reset is not necessarily erasure of the entire institution; V4's objection to systemic resets for local failures is not a ban on every restart. Both repair and retention depend on what is trustworthy and what downstream state has already changed. External records can affect later behavior without changing weights, but beneficial institutional learning requires an actual changed practice and useful later consequences, not growth in logs. C-030's critique of unestablished guarantees is supported; the specifications do not establish a truth oracle, universal instruction isolation, a principal–agent solution or learning merely from permission progression. [R-13 §§5–9](R-13/README.md#5-local-repair-and-reset-are-conditional-alternatives).

These findings support C-026's design rationale without proving its **should**, supplying acceptable-risk thresholds or validating MKS as a package. Reliability work has real costs and may displace useful activity; it should be compared with simpler feasible arrangements at matched quality, risk and resource budgets. Rare, correlated or unobservable failures limit what reassuring averages can establish. Normative unacceptable outcomes can remain hard constraints even when an expected-cost calculation would favor action.

## 6. Contribution, compensation, ownership and welfare

### Good practice and durable rents are different outcomes

Management and organizational studies support bounded productive gains, persistence and failures. They do not identify a universal rent to perpetual redesign. A practice can be teachable and useful without remaining rare; sustained ordinary execution can be better than repeated radical replacement. D-002's “method” needs a specified practice and comparison, rather than defining capability retrospectively as whatever successful institutions did. [R-05, competing explanations](R-05/README.md#competing-explanations-of-advantage).

Architecture, education and operation describe productive activities; ownership describes rights and exposure. A person may combine them, but their returns should not be conflated. Value created can be captured by complementary assets, customers, employers or other workers; remuneration depends on demand, alternatives, bargaining and institutions. Evidence of pay sharing is counterevidence to total employer capture, while productive improvements without matching pay refute proportional-compensation guarantees. The observed separation of work changes and early average wage effects does not establish a permanent wage null. [R-05, compensation](R-05/README.md#from-productive-contribution-to-compensation); [R-09 §5.2](R-09/README.md#52-firm-returns-need-not-become-worker-earnings).

C-040's unconditional ownership strategy is already revised by D-002 and is not restored by this literature. A residual claim can lose money after acquisition, finance, maintenance and competition. Current labor contributions cannot disprove a hypothetical future defined to eliminate all valuable labor, but that definition is not evidence the future will occur. C-041's conditional mechanisms survive; its categorical assertion about every model or workflow for all future time remains unestablished. This is not equivalent to proving every rent disappears. Exact-title wage premiums and a general payoff from role renegotiation remain unresolved. [R-05, claim ledger](R-05/README.md#claim-level-evidence-ledger); [R-12 §7](R-12/README.md#7-owners-workers-and-consumers-can-gain-at-the-same-time).

### Copying, access and rights must be disaggregated before assigning returns

R-15 makes the proposed counterpart an inventory of components and rights, rather than one undifferentiated owned object. A worker, firm or provider can possess some code, license some weights, control access to records and lack other permissions. Joint economic production need not establish equal legal co-ownership or vetoes. Human memory leaving an employer does not erase confidentiality duties; retaining a machine copy need not preserve the worker's judgment or cooperation. Neither economics nor the component-level legal illustration selects a preferred property allocation. [R-15 §§3–6](R-15/README.md#3-what-is-the-proposed-asset).

Technical nonrivalry permits useful sharing, but information can reveal facts about third parties who were absent from the bargain. Conversely, prohibiting all reuse can destroy benefits from genuinely separable authorized components. The relevant comparison concerns investment incentives before development, continuity and performance after separation, distribution and disclosure, not simply who possesses the file. Employer control, worker control, licensed copies, modular separation, provider continuity and collective arrangements remain comparative options. This synthesis selects none. [R-15 §§7–10](R-15/README.md#7-alternative-control-designs-comparative-questions-not-a-recommendation).

The same decomposition corrects C-050. Visual/no-code interfaces are not uniformly proprietary hosted traps; generated raw source does not automatically remove operational dependencies or confer worker rights. Product architecture, license, hosting, export, maintenance and bargaining must be compared separately. The categorical platform claim has counterexamples; net economic superiority and compensation prescriptions remain unestablished. This is a factual constraint, not an author choice between empirically interchangeable stories. [R-02, claim ledger](R-02/README.md#claim-level-evidence-ledger); [R-05, platform comparison](R-05/README.md#no-code-and-raw-code-are-not-ownership-categories).

### Zero price is neither zero value nor missing GDP by definition

C-023–C-025 survive in their conditional form. Useful services need not have a software sale, and market revenue is not welfare. But paid own-account software, commercial open-source activity, unpaid independent volunteering, household enterprises, personal activity, government and nonprofit output have different accounting treatment. A software original and its free copies are different objects. International standards, national implementation and experimental accounts are different vintages. [R-11 §§2–3](R-11/README.md#2-what-must-be-kept-separate).

The valuation debate is therefore partly a disagreement about the question. Resource-cost reconstruction, repeated private replacement, willingness to surrender a service and experienced well-being do not measure the same counterfactual. A stock cannot be added to an annual flow; an old welfare level does not explain a new growth shortfall. Significant nonmarket benefits can coexist with a real measured productivity slowdown. Cheap construction can create many abandoned projects or sustained useful services; representative net additionality and survival are not yet measured for the proposed community-software wave. [R-11 §§5–10](R-11/README.md#5-what-open-source-measurement-actually-establishes).

### Aggregate and distributional outcomes require their own mechanism

Micro gains cannot simply be multiplied by the economy. Adoption, remaining costs, complementary investment, substitution, new tasks and demand determine aggregate effects. Optimistic and adverse wage models differ importantly in capital supply, fixed factors, market power and transition assumptions. Existing-task calibrations omit some possible innovation channels; wider AGI scenarios posit capability paths rather than estimate their probabilities. Their headline forecasts are neither interchangeable nor a statistical confidence interval. [R-12 §§3–6](R-12/README.md#3-what-the-theoretical-disagreement-is-actually-about).

More cognitive-service use need not raise human hours if human input per service falls faster. A lower labor share need not mean lower average real wages, and a rising average need not compensate displaced people. Owners, workers and consumers can gain simultaneously, while particular members of each group lose. Relative service-price declines, disinflation and general deflation are different outcomes, with monetary policy and investment demand affecting the latter. C-045's compulsory surplus sequence, universal rebound and general-deflation inference do not survive as laws. The narrower **likely/probably** prosperity forecast for middle-seat workers also remains unresolved; rejecting a guarantee does not refute or quantify that probability. [R-12 §§4,7–10](R-12/README.md#4-aggregation-requires-demand-and-the-whole-production-network).

## 7. Persistent actors, professional development and paired assets

### Present technical precedents support a conditional future extension

R-14 and R-16 make a necessary two-sided correction. Real parameter adaptation and useful externally retained artifacts exist; describing all current machines as frozen or memoryless is inaccurate. Yet a saved transcript, a repeated-task reflection, a parameter update and a durable professional capability establish different things. External memory can be productive capital even when the foundation model is unchanged. Evidence of that system-level value is not evidence of a uniquely embodied actor advantage. A newer model with the same authorized artifacts is a serious competing explanation, not an unfair baseline. [R-14, taxonomy](R-14/README.md#2-a-persistence-taxonomy-that-prevents-equivocation); [R-14, external-state evidence](R-14/README.md#external-state-can-be-productive-without-weight-learning).

Continual-learning outcomes depend on task identity, replay, retained data, initialization, task order, feedback and cost. Retaining old competence, updating obsolete information, acquiring new competence and remaining able to adapt are separate targets. A method can improve one and regress on another. Comparing a continually updated model with a fresh model reconstructed from the same retained examples can change conclusions, but rebuilding latency must also be counted. Positive and negative findings describe different training problems rather than a settled universal verdict on lifelong learning. [R-14, parameter learning](R-14/README.md#parameter-learning-is-real-but-stability-is-conditional).

The newer adaptation evidence also resists a single burden story. R-14's final SEAL assessment preserves both the main nested training configuration and the alternative proxy-reward and stronger untrained-prompt comparisons. It would be incorrect to generalize one configuration's explicit references, cost or baseline to every parameter-adaptation method. R-16 likewise compares different starting policies, curricula and sampling budgets when assessing whether reinforcement learning expands useful capability. Reweighting already possible behavior can be economically useful; a newly reachable answer can be practically useless if no affordable selector recognizes it. The relevant endpoint is cost-conditioned useful performance, with genuine novelty a separate technical question. [R-14, adaptation comparison](R-14/README.md#self-adaptation-and-test-time-memory-do-not-close-the-lifetime-gap); [R-16, reasoning-boundary debate](R-16/README.md#23-the-reasoning-boundary-disagreement-is-partly-a-measurement-disagreement).

Technical modularity supports some copying without proving clean confidential-information separation or retained value in a new institution. The capital test includes lifecycle services, formation and validation costs, depreciation, compatible operation, permitted reuse and the best replacement alternative. A stronger generic model can depreciate an old customization; a reusable curriculum, tool library or evaluator may be more valuable than a uniquely aged actor. This is a competing location of the productive stock, not a denial that experience can matter. Human-apprenticeship analogies fit the functional need for practice and feedback but break at copying, replay, resets and centralized training. [R-14 §§6–7](R-14/README.md#6-copying-separation-privacy-and-the-location-of-capital); [R-15, portability](R-15/README.md#1-result-the-asset-and-the-right-must-be-specified-separately).

### Development, examination and authorized deployment are separate

For C-035, R-16 distinguishes the learner from the tester. A simulation can train a policy, elicit existing behavior, improve an adversarial test generator, or simply assess an unchanged target. Only some of these develop the actor being discussed. Narrow simulation-to-real transfer and online weight-updating personalization are substantive positives, not occupational validation. The positive findings narrow the evidence gap: the missing chain is durable, actor-specific improvement in consequential work, with retained capabilities, safety and attractive full cost, rather than the mere existence of training. [R-16 §§2–4](R-16/README.md#post-training-is-real-professional-development-is-a-stronger-claim).

A held-out prompt, new simulator seed, changed task family, new harness, physical deployment and unfamiliar institution are progressively different transfer tests. Specifying rare hazards does not cover genuinely unrepresented shocks. Reward improvement can coexist with regressions elsewhere; safety and useful completion must both be tested. Training the stress-test environment can discover a failure without teaching the tested target how to avoid it. Passing a curriculum may inform a permission decision but does not itself confer authority or make the expanded risk exposure equivalent to the old one. [R-16, simulation boundary](R-16/README.md#simulation-transfer-and-the-black-swan-boundary); [R-16, generalization and safety](R-16/README.md#5-generalization-evaluation-leakage-and-unsafe-improvement).

Service interfaces and tariffs document available and changing supply, not lifetime professional competence, representative customer demand or a viable actor-specific market. One service's withdrawal is not failure of the technical method, just as another's availability is not market validation. Bespoke training must be compared with better external state, stronger base models, reusable occupational training and continued use of the current system. The candidate commercial product might be a simulator, evaluator, curriculum, adapter or integration service rather than the continued education of an individually irreplaceable actor. [R-16, service economics](R-16/README.md#service-availability-is-not-market-validation).

### Continuity value is broader than an indivisible pair premium

R-17 reconciles C-036/C-037 by separating productive configuration from control. A firm can own a highly integrated human–AI pair; a worker can operate an essentially autonomous actor; a firm's system can contain both. Thus “pair,” “actor” and “firm-owned” are not mutually exclusive options on one axis. The authored **may** and **depending on setting** survive as a reason to compare nested units, not choose a universal winner. [R-17 §3](R-17/README.md#3-define-the-comparison-before-declaring-a-winner).

The distinction between augmentation and best-component synergy is material. Helping an unaided human does not establish that retaining that human improves over the better solo configuration on the measured task. The meta-analytic record is heterogeneous and design-selected; its aggregate result is not a law against useful combination. Equally, positive synergy in a constructed task does not establish that years of accumulated pairing caused it. Complementary errors or knowledge must be exploited by a suitable interaction or aggregation rule; merely assembling different components is insufficient. Legitimate human authorization or another valuable constraint can favor a configuration without creating measured accuracy synergy. [R-17 §4](R-17/README.md#4-assistance-complementarity-and-actual-synergy); [R-07 §6](R-07/README.md#6-authority-is-a-property-of-the-institution-as-well-as-the-interface).

History can contribute through the human's calibration, transferable profiles, reusable tools, initial matching, mutual adaptation or organizational access. These mechanisms have different precedents and limitations. A style match can improve a short collaboration before any relationship history exists. A practiced operator can learn a system's error boundary, then lose that calibration after an otherwise beneficial upgrade. Rapport, preference and engagement need not improve economically valuable work. Repeated interaction can reinforce false assumptions as well as good practice. Tenure, saved-token volume or subjective attachment therefore do not measure beneficial compounding. [R-17 §§5–7](R-17/README.md#5-what-relationship-history-might-contain).

Two counterfactuals must coexist. The **total continuity effect** includes the records, tools and human practices that continuity helped create. Giving those artifacts to a fresh system removes part of that treatment, so failure to find a remaining premium would not show the original investment was worthless. The **residual match-specific effect** asks whether the same human and particular counterpart still outperform well-informed replacements after legitimate state transfer and realistic onboarding. Equalized-state comparisons are appropriate for that narrower question. Temporary disruption after a switch is not necessarily a durable relational asset. R-14's internal-state versus whole-system comparison and R-17's two estimands thus reinforce rather than contradict each other. [R-14, retained-change tests](R-14/README.md#r14-t1--locate-the-retained-change); [R-17 §11](R-17/README.md#11-discriminating-comparison-designs).

An executable résumé adds a further bridge. A live demonstration, a lawfully portable productive bundle and a validated signal predicting future job performance are different accomplishments. Ordinary work-history and résumé evidence establishes adjacent information mechanisms, not a counterpart credential. Authenticity, preparation, available complements, attribution, repeated performance, destination fit and lawful disclosure must be established. A hiring premium could reflect clearer signaling or access to superior resources rather than durable pair productivity; it need not represent aggregate welfare or a permanent wage return. C-037 remains unresolved in this full scope. [R-17 §§9–10](R-17/README.md#9-the-executable-résumé-work-sample-portable-system-or-market-signal); [R-15 §§8–10](R-15/README.md#8-a-departure-scenario-that-reveals-the-missing-evidence).

## 8. Historical examples: correction before rhetorical choice

The historical audit changes the permissible use of examples without deciding which examples the author should retain.

- **Portugal and Methuen:** relative-cost reasoning survives; numerical examples are hypothetical, treaty access and tariff conditions are specific, and exchange gains are distinct from development. Wine production, stocks, infrastructure, finance and market relationships invalidate an absolute wine-versus-capital or national consumption-versus-investment dichotomy. Vineyard productivity at the asserted dates and a treaty-specific development counterfactual remain unresolved. National labels conceal heterogeneous participants and colonial coercion. [Portugal, assertion ledger](R-18/portugal.md#claim-level-evidence-ledger).
- **Ford:** task-time improvement, compensation policy, price paths, workweek changes and modern personnel reporting have different dates and denominators. An assembly station designed to reduce discretion is not evidence that an AI review station educates judgment. A reported hiring-and-mentoring response does not isolate the effect of rehiring a previously AI-displaced cohort. Relative affordability does not establish general deflation or universal household prosperity. [Ford, assertion ledger](R-18/ford-surplus.md#7-claim-level-evidence-ledger).
- **Electrification:** unit drive and complementary reorganization are historically grounded. The simple exclusive layout explanation and universal lag are not. Engineering history, local exposure, national productivity and patent generality measure different outcomes. [Electricity, methods and judgments](R-18/biography-electricity.md#5-electrification-the-mechanism-survives-the-simple-delay-story-does-not).
- **Jones and selected biographies:** the burden mechanism and its evidence are recoverable, but patent proxies do not isolate the general cause of specialization. Jones permits easing burdens; priority for the specific proposed AI hybrid still requires its own argument. Rogan's chronology and bundled ownership portrayal need correction; expert exposure does not demonstrate broad validated expertise. Leonardo's apprenticeship, institutions and exceptional practice cannot identify a population treatment effect. Ricardo's literal lifetime negative remains unverified. Naming decisions remain the author's. [Biographies and Jones](R-18/biography-electricity.md#3-jones-what-was-actually-measured-and-what-remains-an-extension).
- **AI buildout and prior booms:** the news lead and narrow scenario arithmetic are recovered. The comparison concerns investment intensity across selected, differently defined episodes, not combined technological effects. The historical ranking remains unharmonized; a financing scenario and required-revenue calculation are not realized spending or estimated demand. Useful infrastructure and investor losses can coexist. Ownership, use and downside exposure may fall on different parties. [Buildout, evidence and limits](R-18/buildout.md#evidence-and-arithmetic).

Corrections do not require a new author vote on arithmetic, chronology or formal definitions. The author's choices concern explanatory role, emphasis and which bounded cases best serve the intended argument. An unresolved historical magnitude cannot be made true by choosing to retain the vignette. Conversely, failure of an inflated retelling need not remove every mechanism the case can illustrate.

## 10. What remains uncertain, and what belongs to the author

### Load-bearing empirical uncertainties

The reviews identify specific unresolved bridges rather than an undifferentiated request for more research:

- Representative full-cost useful-task trends and the causal scale of new worthwhile activity, including failed attempts and nonadopters.
- Comparative returns to redesigned versus incrementally improved workflows over maintenance, model changes and institutional turnover.
- Retained independent human judgment, far transfer and future mentor production under alternative AI work/training arrangements.
- Net senior-capability stocks and the complete organizational chain linking staffing choices, current correction, successor development and later rebuilding costs.
- Persistent net advantage from a specified redesign method, and the incidence of its gains in actual compensation and rights.
- Current household/community software formation, useful survival, beneficiaries and accounting coverage.
- Long-run useful-service elasticities, macro adoption and capital adjustment, new activities, transition losses and distribution across households and countries.
- Rare consequential reliability, truthful evidence, correlated checking and governance costs in realistic long-run systems.
- Durable actor-specific learning, safe transfer, clean separability, paired-history effects and credible executable credentials, with the narrower technical components already demonstrated.

Public source non-recovery has a narrower meaning: an inaccessible final version, unreplicated restricted data, absent personnel history or unsuccessful public corroboration does not prove no effect or no record exists. The reviews have bounded their assertions accordingly. Conversely, a categorical logical or definitional error does not require a future dataset before it can be corrected.

### Priority questions for the next author refinement

These are decisions and clarifications for Luke after reviewing the evidence, not new research execution, revised decisions adopted on his behalf, or a request to reopen D-001/D-002.

**First: define the present argument and its obligations.**

1. **What is the central outcome and scope?** Should the present argument chiefly explain more useful completed work at acceptable full cost, broader productive participation, preservation of future capability, or a combination with explicitly separate measures? Which beneficiaries and time horizon should its strongest claims cover? This determines which evidence is load-bearing without selecting a final thesis here.
2. **How should the economic terms be used?** Will “production function” be an explicitly operational metaphor or be reserved for the formal relation? Does “new factor” mean a provisionable service or a stronger category claim? Does “simultaneous specialization” refer to assisted productive breadth, retained human expertise, or competence of the human–tool system? These choices cannot erase the distinctions already established.
3. **Within D-001, which independent capabilities must be preserved and demonstrated?** What must a person recognize, explain, reconstruct or escalate before taking consequential responsibility, and under what degraded-tool conditions? The manual-foundations commitment stands; the question is its domain-sensitive expression and what evidence would justify changing dosage or sequence, not whether the author still accepts it.
4. **Which human authority is a commitment independent of comparative performance?** Which decisions should remain human-authorized, at what level, and which consequences are unacceptable even if a narrow productivity measure improves? Evidence can inform the cost and feasibility of those choices but cannot decide legitimacy or risk tolerance.

**Next: set the strength of economic and future claims.**

5. **Within D-002, how strong should the promise to workers be?** Should the book describe possible productive and income routes, recommend conditional strategies, or make a narrower institutional argument? The current position already rejects guaranteed pay and ownership dominance. Any career prescription needs a stated population, horizon and relevant outside options.
6. **What observable meaning should “the method is the moat” carry?** A repeatedly useful practice, persistent relative performance and economic rent are different claims. Over what interval and against what alternatives should durable advantage be claimed, and what outcome would make the author revise it?
7. **Which distributional objectives should the book defend?** Higher total welfare, broader productive access, worker development, income security, fair sharing and greater control are not interchangeable. Research supplies trade-offs; it does not select a policy package or a property allocation.
8. **How much of the future extension should bear the book's argument?** Should persistent learned actors and paired capital appear as bounded possibilities, a scenario comparison, or a more ambitious research-dependent argument? No single universal productive unit is required by C-036. “Executable résumé” needs a declared target: demonstration, attribution of contribution, portable capability or a predictive hiring credential.
9. **Which rights and third-party interests should guide the unresolved allocation problem?** Before choosing worker, firm, shared or provider-mediated control, which objectives matter at entry, during development and on departure? The review identifies alternatives without choosing among them; technical copying alone cannot answer the question.

**Finally: select explanatory examples and the public case study's burden.**

10. **Which corrected historical or biographical examples still earn their place?** Portugal, Ford, electrification, Rogan, Leonardo and buildout can perform different limited roles. The choice belongs to the author after the factual corrections and uncertainty are retained. No example is selected here.
11. **What should the public book process demonstrate?** An inspectable provenance and correction process can support a bounded case study of institutional functions. What comparative outcomes, costs and failure record would be needed before it carries an effectiveness claim? Public version history alone cannot validate the book's whole economic thesis or reveal complete internal reasoning.

These questions are ordered to avoid asking for a polished thesis before its units, limits and values are explicit. They permit author refinement while keeping empirical unknowns empirical. They do not authorize drafting chapters, modifying protected history or commencing new experiments.

## 11. Method, source ownership and completion boundary

This is a second-order analytical synthesis of the public review corpus and admitted authored records. It follows mechanisms and counterarguments across review bodies, claim ledgers and access/method appendices. The sources remain owned by their reviews: readers should follow the linked capsules for exact populations, denominators, dates, identification, sponsor disclosures and access limits. No source-specific numerical headline is repeated as a new finding here, and multiple programs using the same study do not create additional independent evidence.

The campaign is purposive rather than a preregistered exhaustive systematic review. It combines formal theory, conceptual scholarship, experiments, quasi-experiments, observational records, reconstructed history, official accounting/legal text, technical benchmarks and attributed organizational testimony. These methods answer different questions. A model gives conditional implications; a narrow trial gives an effect for its tested intervention; a standard defines a convention; a biography supplies a selected example. Source prestige or primary status does not remove selection, measurement or transport limits. Neither underlying restricted data nor full model implementations were independently replicated in the synthesis.

The main transport hazards are changing model and interface vintages, short observation windows, selected sophisticated users, bundled support, variable outcome definitions, sparse independent end-to-end validation and concentrations in particular countries or industries. Better contemporary evidence can change quantitative conclusions without eliminating the definitional distinctions. Absence of a general effect estimate is not absence of every bounded causal finding.

**Completion discipline:** independent review of this synthesis and the 46-row matrix passed. Structural, protected-state and remote-verification results are recorded in the [completion record](../../method/LOOP-2-COMPLETION.md). No thesis is frozen, chapter drafted, preferred historical example chosen or property allocation adopted. The terminal result is reportable only after authoritative remote verification.
