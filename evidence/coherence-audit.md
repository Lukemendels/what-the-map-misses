# Coherence and conflict audit

Checkpoint C. All ten admitted sources were read in full; no external source was retrieved. This is conceptual adjudication of what the corpus supports, not factual verification. Claim paths and locators are in `evidence/claims/CLAIM-LEDGER.md`; S-IDs are in the manifest. Source wording remains untouched.

There are 18 audit cases: 2 reinforcing formulations, 2 evolution/supersession cases, 4 resolved tensions, 8 material ambiguities assigned to research, and 2 authorial conflicts with pending packets. Counts refer to cases, not individual sentences. Research ambiguity means the corpus cannot establish a definition, causal bridge or factual assertion; it is not necessarily a demand that Luke choose a belief. No authorial conflict was resolved by this audit.

## A-01 — Faster input versus redesigned production

**Classification: Reinforcing formulations.** C-003–C-008/C-043. S-03's stack, S-04's factory and AutoReviewer, and S-10 §§2–3 reinforce an input-versus-arrangement distinction. Do not duplicate the same argument as separate discoveries merely because its metaphors change. The historical analogy is unverified (R-04); the model/code division has its own authored example. The analogy breaks because AI can assist in redesigning its own machinery and because information/authority are not physical belts. Neither a thirty-year AI delay nor redesign as the sole cause of productivity follows.

## A-02 — Structural human monopoly versus adaptive cognition

**Classification: Evolution / supersession.** C-010/C-011/C-047. S-01's “structurally zero” and “no amount of compute” are stronger than a training-data-only limitation; S-03 repeats “We will always hold the wine.” S-06 challenges this as an observation-cost asymmetry and explicitly labels defenses provisional. S-10 §4 says “I no longer want the book to depend on an eternal human monopoly over any fixed category of cognition.” That is affirmative revision evidence, not mere recency. Preserve C-010 as superseded in permanent scope; retain C-011 as a testable present claim. The audit does not establish that machines will acquire every form of judgment. R-06/R-14 must test both erosion and the defenses, including unobserved counterfactuals, relocation and consent. No author packet is needed to ask Luke to repeat an explicit revision.

## A-03 — Fixed middle seat versus moving human placement

**Classification: Resolvable tension, resolved.** C-012–C-014. S-01/S-02 derive a particular workflow from a particular contextual advantage. S-04's “The point is not to preserve a human checkpoint everywhere” and “The stations are moving,” reinforced by S-10 §§3–4, supply the reconciliation: fast/human/fast can be useful where situated judgment is necessary; it is not the universal future topology. This narrows the scope of the early prescription explicitly rather than disguising its stronger language. Human authority must be exercisable, not merely assigned on paper. The normative case for responsibility survives changing capability; it does not prove economic scarcity. D-001 separately addresses whether such work develops judgment.

## A-04 — Where do future foremen learn?

**Classification: Authorial conflict.** C-015–C-018. S-02 says output supervision does not provide the underlying texture and requires reps doing the actual cognitive work. S-05 calls review-only apprenticeship severed. S-03 says station work matures into the territory-rich foreman role for those who treat it as apprenticeship. S-10 §5 again says deliberate redesign is necessary rather than assumed reproduction. No text explains what extra activity converts the station into adequate training, nor explicitly withdraws S-03's promotion mechanism. Both strong causal accounts cannot be the default account unchanged. D-001 asks whether to retain that mechanism, reject it for ordinary stations, or author a conditional synthesis. R-08 can test training designs, but adding unmentioned training to S-03 is an authorial revision, not an accomplished reconciliation.

## A-05 — Human apprenticeship versus automation and machine development

**Classification: Resolvable tension, resolved.** C-015/C-019/C-021/C-033/C-035. S-02 requires actual cognitive reps, expert exposure and consequences. S-05 explicitly revises an execution/judgment split: AI as tutor and workshop can accelerate learning through doing; it insists “compressed, not zero.” S-10 §5 rejects preserving every obsolete manual task. Reconcile at the level of developmental function: active practice with feedback can coexist with automation; passive acceptance is not equivalent practice. This does not prove AI-assisted practice matches unaided competence. Human embodied learning, institutional record revision (C-031), and hypothetical adaptive machine learning (C-033) remain distinct mechanisms. Investing in human development is not logically inconsistent with later developing machines. No universal training recipe or success guarantee is inferred; D-001 remains open for ordinary station work.

## A-06 — Specialize versus span domains

**Classification: Resolvable tension, resolved.** C-020/C-021/C-048. S-05's final tension explicitly distinguishes specialization in territory-grounded judgment from executing in one occupational domain. S-10 §6 allows native depth and adjacent competence through projects. The unit changes from domain label to the function of judgment, and the seed itself explains the change. The refinement admits new adjacent judgment rather than an immovable native/foreign split. Do not claim everyone becomes a polymath or that acquisition cost is the only historical driver: S-05's strong exclusion and “first technology” assertion require R-10. Jones, da Vinci and Rogan are proposed illustrations, not verified causal evidence. The deliberate Rogan naming decision remains source provenance; Loop 1 does not reverse it or canonize an unwritten chapter.

## A-07 — Restriction/reset versus trust/local repair

**Classification: Evolution / supersession.** C-026–C-029. V3 explicitly keeps redraft and termination as competing hypotheses. V4 §1.1 explicitly “pivots” to enablement; §2.3 rejects systemic resets in response to localized failure. This is actual policy development, not a contradiction to ask Luke to resolve again. Bounded authority, durable state, review and enforcement persist; universal strict vetoes, particular envelopes and triune structures are historical proposals, not mandatory current machinery. V4 still imposes a ruin barrier, so trust is not unrestricted autonomy. Its local-failure rejection does not establish that restarting is never appropriate. S-10 §8 explicitly distinguishes enduring functions from possibly overbuilt implementation. Technical efficacy and risk classification remain R-13, not settled by the specification's normative tone.

## A-08 — Guaranteed truth and RLHF versus bounded error and external learning

**Classification: Material ambiguity (research).** C-026/C-030–C-035. V3 says it solves the principal-agent problem and turns a credence good into a search good through telemetry. V4 claims reality filtering, contamination-free skill routing and RLHF authority progression. Neither source supplies measurements or the machinery needed to establish those guarantees. S-10 §§8–11 shifts the target to bounded propagation and explicitly distinguishes external institutional learning from an actor materially changed by experience. A deterministic gate can enforce a predicate without establishing that its inputs describe truth; a trust score or permission increase is not evidence of weight learning. These are unsupported technical bridges to test under R-13/R-14/R-16. The candidate carries the design objective, not the guarantee. No proof that all these systems work is inferred, and no technical falsity is declared from intuition alone.

## A-09 — Ownership as a dominance prescription

**Classification: Authorial conflict.** C-009/C-039–C-041/C-050. S-06 requires a steelman without a pre-installed escape hatch, then makes ownership the one winning position under either human-moat outcome. S-03 distinguishes an architect's high-skilled wage from actual ownership and explicitly names owner risk. S-07 Phase 3 says competition destroys abnormal profits. S-04 denies permanent differentiation from one workflow and emphasizes recurring redesign; S-10 §§4,7,13 conditions capture on production design, value distinctions and rights. Both a universal safe strategic destination and a contingent competitive account cannot serve as the unchanged conclusion. Neither S-06's parked status nor S-03's retirement proves withdrawal of ownership dominance. D-002 isolates this fork; a dynamic-owner synthesis is available only as a proposal Luke must adopt. R-05/R-12/R-15 can narrow factual conditions but cannot choose the book's advice.

## A-10 — Surplus law versus conditional welfare and measurement

**Classification: Material ambiguity (research).** C-023–C-025/C-045. S-07's three phases, elastic-demand treadmill and deflation, and S-03's rising living standards are far stronger than S-10 §7's conditional macro hypotheses. The current synthesis expressly demands research, but does not explicitly retract each historical generalization. Preserve them as unverified predecessor hypotheses rather than declare them superseded or silently turn them into facts. Firm output, worker pay, capital rents, consumer surplus and GDP are different quantities. No transaction does not establish no GDP recognition; low production cost does not imply low service value or monopoly rent. R-11/R-12 must resolve measurement and equilibrium bridges. The narrow conditional thesis does not depend on a universal phase law.

## A-11 — What does writable production function mean?

**Classification: Material ambiguity (research).** C-004/C-006. S-04 explicitly uses production function to mean the arrangement of work; S-10 adds governance and control rights. The operational meaning is recoverable; its relation to formal input-output technology, organizational choice and technical change remains underspecified. “New factor” also needs economic precision about services versus assets. Preserve the authored phrase with its operational definition and mark R-03; do not silently install a formal model or require Luke to choose one before researching the conceptual distinctions. If later precision reveals a substantive fork, later work must escalate it.

## A-12 — Cheap machinery versus expensive usable systems

**Classification: Material ambiguity (research).** C-001/C-002/C-007/C-008/C-022/C-049–C-051. S-07's language-to-code and no-code-trap prescription appears to bypass expertise; S-04 V3 explicitly adds mixed developer results and prototype/hardening caveats and reframes the claim around the existence threshold. The narrower feasibility interpretation is directly supported, but actual costs, generality and effect sizes are unknown. Adoption numbers, the dozen-browser-tool testimony and AutoReviewer failure illustrate possibilities; neither selected testimony nor draft links establish population effects. Cheap creation → secure sustained use → net productive gain requires multiple tests. Preserve maintenance, accessibility, integration and inherited-control failure modes. R-01/R-02/R-04/R-10 must test outcomes, not count impressive outputs.

## A-13 — Institution-specific knowledge versus hiring it back

**Classification: Material ambiguity (research).** C-017/C-019/C-044. S-02 says senior knowledge cannot be bought in the market and cannot be regrown, then predicts hiring costly expertise from competitors. Institutional specificity can mean incomplete transfer rather than impossibility, but that softer account does not preserve both absolutes unchanged. This is an empirical question about transfer, rebuilding times, training complements and labor supply (R-08/R-09), not a choice about Luke's values. Retain both original assertions in provenance; candidate uses the supported risk of costly loss and demands deliberate reproduction, not literal irreversible extinction or guaranteed salary windfalls. S-03's alleged Ford rehiring is a lead, not corroboration.

## A-14 — Labor, software, learned experience and control rights

**Classification: Material ambiguity (research).** C-033/C-034/C-038/C-039. S-10 §§10,13 explicitly opens copyability, transfer, joint ownership and confidentiality questions. ## A-17 — Ricardo's opening and closing rules

**Classification: Material ambiguity (research; internal theoretical inconsistency).** C-046. These formulations are not equivalent on the corpus's own terms. R-03/R-18 must audit the economics and historical analogy. This is a correctable theoretical assertion, not an authorial choice between values; preserve both and withhold it as a premise for C-053. Do not rewrite the published essay or derive future labor extinction from the analogy.
