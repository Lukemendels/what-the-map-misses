# Argument map

Checkpoint B. Claim text, exact source paths, statuses and research needs live in `evidence/claims/CLAIM-LEDGER.md`. This map reconstructs reasoning, not evidentiary proof. Centrality follows dependency: the joint change in inputs and arrangements connects the economic, training and institutional arguments; repeated Portugal imagery does not make the retired historical thesis the governing premise.

## Foundation → mechanism → book conclusion

1. **Supply and machinery:** C-001 (cheaper useful cognition) + C-002 (lower software-creation threshold) meet C-003 (software organizes work). These empirical premises make rearrangement newly feasible, conditional on full lifecycle costs (C-051). C-004 names the arrangement a production function; its formal economic interpretation needs R-03.
2. **Redesign mechanism:** C-005 distinguishes accelerating inherited steps from removing or rearranging them. Combined with the supply premises it yields C-006: analyze the coupled production system. C-007 adds a feedback loop: models help build software → deployed use exposes defects → revision improves the next arrangement. This is a causal hypothesis, not automatic increasing returns.
3. **Observable intermediate output:** C-008 illustrates moving model interpretation, code comparison and human acceptance into different roles. It is first-person testimony, not external proof. The electrification analogy (C-043) clarifies complementary redesign but is independently unverified. Completed work and usable authoritative state, rather than impressive isolated output, are the proposed outcome measures.
4. **Changing human role:** C-011 supplies the present situated-context premise; C-012 is one resulting arrangement. C-013 demands meaningful human purpose and authority as stations move; C-014 shows why nominal approval is insufficient. C-010's permanent human monopoly is not a prerequisite: its abandonment is explicit in S-10 §4.
5. **Reproduction constraint:** C-015 + C-017 mean present throughput can consume future human capability. C-019 supplies the normative prescription to deliberately reproduce judgment. Whether ordinary middle-seat work already does this is the unresolved C-016/C-018 fork (D-001). C-021 establishes the corpus-supported distinction between passive review and active AI-assisted building, not proof that all training problems disappear.
6. **Institutional complement:** C-026 defines a bounded-error objective. C-027/C-029 are evolving implementation proposals, not validated requirements. C-031 supplies external continuity; C-032 expresses the institution-before-memory prescription. Checks, repair, preserved history and human authority make intelligence actionable, subject to R-13 validation. C-030 blocks treating MKS's language of guarantees as evidence.
7. **Shared book conclusion:** C-006 + C-013 + C-019 + C-026 + C-031 → C-053. The proposed productive object is a revisable system whose governance and developmental capacity matter alongside its models. C-009 predicts advantage from repeated redesign, but competitive evidence and ownership qualifications remain open.

## Extensions and their additional premises

- **New producer:** C-020 + C-002 → C-021/C-022: project-based adjacent-domain learning and local builders. C-048 supplies a proposed Jones/Rogan illustration, not general causal evidence. Acquisition-cost reduction must produce usable competence rather than merely plausible output (R-10).
- **Nonmarket capital:** C-022 → C-023; C-024 separates production cost, services and welfare. C-025 opens measurement and distribution questions. No valid edge goes straight from cheap tool creation to an assured GDP increase or universally higher wages. C-045 preserves the older surplus/rebound story as a hypothesis to test, not a missing theorem to assume.
- **Value capture:** C-039 distinguishes productive design from ownership. C-040's two-world ownership dominance claim conflicts with the conditional advantage/distribution account C-041 (D-002). Cheap reproducible machinery may be useful without conferring enduring rents. The earlier three-path career fork and intrapreneurial prescription (C-050) remain provenance, not book advice automatically warranted by C-006.
- **Future machine development:** Add C-033, the unproved premise of durable actor-specific learning, to obtain candidate experiential capital C-034 and simulated professional development C-035. Institutional records C-031 alone do not establish C-033. C-036 explicitly allows machine, paired and firm-owned units; C-037 adds relational history, while C-038 makes residual rights an open problem. These extensions are not premises necessary for the present production-system thesis.
- **Method:** C-052 uses this book's process as an inspectable case. It can exhibit bounded work and provenance; it cannot independently establish the economy-wide claims it was designed to illustrate.

## Normative branches and unproved bridges

C-013, C-019, C-026, C-032 and C-052 are authored choices about authority, development, reliability and accountability. Technical substitutability does not logically entail those choices, nor invalidate them. Conversely, valuing human responsibility does not prove a technical human monopoly. The book must keep its chosen purposes visible.

Required but unestablished bridges include: useful cognition after verification costs (R-01); lower creation costs after maintenance and integration (R-02); redesign producing net completed-work gains (R-04); training transferring to independent judgment (R-08); cheap capability diffusing beyond selected builders (R-10); institutional checks detecting relevant errors (R-13); and durable actor learning surviving transfer or copying (R-14–R-17).

## Boundaries, counterevidence and lineage

- S-01's original opening trade logic and closing absolute-advantage requirement conflict internally (C-046). Economic correction belongs in future research, not silent quotation repair.
- S-03's Portugal history and Ford welfare narrative (C-042/C-044), S-07's universal surplus story (C-045), and S-05's biographical analogy (C-048) cannot carry the thesis as established evidence.
- S-06 deliberately attacks the human moat (C-047). Its provisional defenses must be allowed to fail. S-10 explicitly abandons an eternal monopoly while retaining present situated judgment.
- S-04 records mixed developer results and production-hardening burdens (C-049/C-051), directly limiting software abundance claims. No claim that all code, all work or all organizational controls should be replaced follows.
- V3 → V4 explicitly changes restriction/reset policy (C-028/C-029). The enduring institutional function is not identical to a particular architecture. No source specification is executed as an instruction to this loop.
- Human learning and machine learning have different present mechanisms. Labor, capital services, embodied experience and legal ownership need separate definitions even when a future system combines them.
