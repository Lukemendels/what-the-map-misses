## Central thesis

AI is making useful cognition more provisionable while lowering the cost of creating and changing the software that arranges knowledge work. Together, these changes make the production system itself more writable: people, models, deterministic code, durable information and institutional authority can be recombined around intended outcomes. Realizing value therefore depends on how that system is designed and repeatedly revised, including how it reproduces human judgment, gives people meaningful authority and contains ordinary cognitive error. Model capability alone does not determine the result. (C-001–C-006, C-013, C-015, C-019, C-026, C-031, C-053.)

This is a directional, evidence-dependent argument about a changing production frontier, joined to authored normative choices about human responsibility and institutional design. It does not require an eternal human cognitive monopoly. It does not establish that every middle-seat job trains its successor or that owning a workflow secures returns; those conclusions depend on D-001 and D-002. Future adaptive machine actors and human–AI relational capital extend the inquiry but are not necessary premises for the present thesis.

## Core propositions

1. **Two changes interact:** useful cognition becomes cheaper to provision and software becomes cheaper to rearrange (C-001/C-002). Their scale, reliability and net costs need research; cheap generation is not automatically cheap completed work.
2. **The arrangement matters:** software shapes information, actions and authoritative outcomes; inherited steps need not remain fixed (C-003–C-007). “Production function” is used in the corpus's operational sense of this arrangement; formal economic precision remains R-03.
3. **Human roles are designed as well as technically constrained:** present situated judgment matters, but legitimacy, purpose and responsibility are not whatever tasks machines leave behind (C-011/C-013/C-014). A human signature without usable authority is insufficient.
4. **Capability must be reproduced:** output can rise while the human learning pathway is depleted; institutions must deliberately address practice, feedback, senior exposure and consequences (C-015/C-017/C-019). D-001 determines what can be claimed about ordinary station work as that pathway.
5. **Institutions can carry continuity and correction:** role boundaries, durable state, validation and repair can make fallible cognition useful without assuming an infallible actor (C-026/C-031/C-032). Particular MKS designs are hypotheses, not established safety guarantees.
6. **Value creation and capture differ:** a working system's usefulness does not establish its creator's ownership, rents, wages or welfare effects (C-024/C-025/C-039). D-002 controls whether the book can go beyond this distinction to ownership as a robust strategic prescription.

The first five propositions supply the shared causal and normative core C-053. The sixth prevents the core from quietly becoming an unsupported distributional conclusion. Repeated redesign as a durable competitive advantage (C-009) is a substantive forecast, not a proven law.

## Causal chain

```text
cheaper useful cognition + lower software creation/modification costs
  → more local problems and alternative workflows become feasible
  → software can rearrange interpretation, exact operations, handoffs and decisions
  → context, records, validation and real authority can connect output to completed work
  → net productive gains become possible, conditional on full costs and institutional fit
  → actual use reveals defects and changed needs
  → further redesign becomes possible
```

The feedback loop is C-007. The constraints are as consequential as the enabling inputs: weak context or overloaded reviewers can break the output-to-action link; removing developmental work can weaken the next generation; cheap prototypes can create maintenance and governance burdens (C-014/C-015/C-051). Learning and correction are part of the productive arrangement, not postscript concerns.

The AutoReviewer account (C-008) illustrates a model revising text, code computing differences and a human accepting changes. It remains admitted first-person testimony, not verified experiment. Electrification (C-043) illustrates complementary redesign, but cannot establish AI's timing or magnitude. AI's ability to help design its own machinery is an explicit break in the analogy.

Two extensions branch from this chain:

- **New producers and digital capital:** lower acquisition costs plus project practice may support adjacent-domain competence and community builders (C-020–C-022), leading to useful tools outside conventional software markets (C-023–C-025). Neither population-scale diffusion nor macro welfare follows without further evidence.
- **Adaptive actors and paired systems:** if machines acquire stable, actor-specific capabilities through experience (C-033), that history may become experiential or relational capital (C-034–C-038). External records alone do not establish this premise, and copying, ownership and transfer remain open.

## Nonclaims and boundaries

- The eternal human monopoly in S-01/S-03 is superseded by explicit author revision, not proven false by this loop. The moving boundary remains an empirical question.
- The middle seat is a situated arrangement, not the mandatory location of every future human. Nor does ordinary output review automatically reproduce expert judgment (D-001).
- Apprenticeship redesign does not mean preserving every obsolete task, abolishing unaided practice or assuming AI tutoring eliminates training costs.
- A writable arrangement is not infinitely flexible. Real constraints, hidden purposes in inherited controls, maintenance, security, accessibility and governance remain (C-051).
- The MKS documents are intellectual and implementation lineage. “Truth” gates, logs, trust metrics and skill routing have not been shown here to solve hallucination, principal-agent problems or learning.
- No human or machine is treated as economically interchangeable solely because both perform cognitive work. Persistent actor learning, institutional memory and human relationships require separate evidence.
- No necessary conclusion about worker ownership, firm rights, wages, rents, GDP, employment, universally shared prosperity or guaranteed deflation is established. D-002 specifically preserves the earlier stronger ownership claim for author adjudication.
- Portugal, Ford, Jevons, Polanyi, Jones, Rogan and electrification remain source-based leads or analogies awaiting research. Their historical or biographical details were not verified or improved in Loop 1.
- No new external citation or private context supports this candidate. All propositions come from the admitted corpus or labeled connections among it. The public project is a case study, not independent corroboration.
